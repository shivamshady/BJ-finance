What is a Top-Up Loan?

Additional Funds on Your Existing Loan

A top-up loan is an additional loan offered to eligible customers who already have an active loan with a lender. It may be considered when a customer requires additional funds without taking an entirely new loan from a different lender.

ℹ️ Top-up loan availability, amount, interest rate and terms are decided solely by the respective bank or NBFC based on your current loan status, repayment history and their applicable policies.

General Eligibility Considerations

  • Active existing loan with a bank or NBFC
  • Satisfactory repayment history on the existing loan
  • Remaining loan tenure sufficient as per lender policy
  • Income and employment stability assessment
  • FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) within acceptable limits

* Eligibility is determined by the respective lender. BJ FINANCES assists with the application process only.

Common Reasons for Top-Up

  • Additional funds required without a new loan application
  • Convenience of a single EMI arrangement (in some cases)
  • Existing lender relationship may expedite processing
  • Home renovation or emergency expenses
  • Education or medical requirements

FAQ

Top-Up Loan FAQs

Can I get a top-up from a different lender? ▾
Yes, some lenders offer a top-up loan along with a balance transfer. This means you switch your existing loan to a new lender while also taking additional funds. Share your details and our team will discuss the options available to you.
How much top-up can I get? ▾
The top-up amount depends on the lender's policy, your current outstanding amount, repayment history, income, and overall eligibility assessment. BJ FINANCES will help you understand your options.
Does BJ FINANCES guarantee top-up approval? ▾
No. All loan approvals, amounts and terms are decided solely by the respective bank or NBFC. BJ FINANCES assists with the application and facilitation process only.
