Understanding OD

What is an Overdraft Facility?

An Overdraft (OD) facility is a type of credit arrangement offered by banks and financial institutions to eligible customers. It allows the account holder to withdraw funds beyond their account balance, up to a pre-approved limit sanctioned by the lender.

Unlike a fixed-term loan, interest on an OD facility is typically calculated only on the amount actually utilised, and for the duration it is utilised — though terms vary by lender.

ℹ️ OD facility terms, interest rates, approved limits and charges are determined solely by the respective bank or NBFC. BJ FINANCES assists eligible customers in exploring and applying for OD facilities.

Key Features (Generally)

  • Access funds up to an approved limit as required
  • Interest typically charged on the amount utilised
  • Flexible repayment — repay as and when funds are available (subject to lender terms)
  • Facility can be renewed or reviewed by the lender periodically
  • Suitable for managing short-term liquidity requirements

* Features, interest rates and terms vary by lender. BJ FINANCES does not determine OD terms.

General Eligibility Considerations

  • Salaried employees with stable employment history
  • Existing relationship with the bank (in some cases)
  • Credit score and repayment history
  • Income and employment stability

* Eligibility criteria are determined by the respective lender. Final approval is at the lender's discretion.

FAQ

Overdraft FAQs

Is an OD facility a loan? ▾
An OD facility is a credit arrangement rather than a fixed term loan. It allows you to withdraw funds up to an approved limit, with interest charged on the amount utilised. Specific terms depend on the lender.
Do I pay interest on the full OD limit? ▾
Generally, interest on an OD facility is calculated on the amount utilised and for the period of utilisation, not on the full sanctioned limit. However, terms vary by lender and should be confirmed before availing the facility.
Can BJ FINANCES guarantee an OD facility? ▾
No. BJ FINANCES assists eligible customers in applying for an OD facility. Approval, limit, rate and terms are determined solely by the respective bank or NBFC.
Who is eligible for an OD facility? ▾
