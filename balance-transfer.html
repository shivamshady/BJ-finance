What is Balance Transfer?

Move Your Loan for Potentially Better Terms

A balance transfer involves moving an existing loan from your current lender to another lender. This may be considered when a customer wishes to explore more suitable repayment terms, subject to eligibility and lender approval.

⚠️ A balance transfer decision should consider interest-rate differences, outstanding balance, remaining tenure and any applicable switching costs. BJ FINANCES recommends evaluating all factors before proceeding.

Who May Benefit?

  • Customers currently paying relatively high interest rates
  • Customers with existing personal loans looking for better repayment options
  • Customers seeking potential EMI reduction over remaining tenure
  • Customers interested in a top-up along with balance transfer
  • Customers with improved credit profile since original loan

Key Considerations

📊

Interest Rate Difference

The potential benefit depends on the rate difference between current and proposed lender.

📅

Remaining Tenure

Longer remaining tenure may provide more benefit, but this varies by situation.

💰

Switching Costs

Processing fees, foreclosure charges and other costs should be factored into your decision.

✅

Eligibility Reassessment

The new lender will independently assess your current eligibility based on their policies.

FAQ

Balance Transfer FAQs

Will a balance transfer definitely reduce my EMI? ▾
Not necessarily. EMI reduction depends on the interest rate difference, outstanding balance, remaining tenure and any applicable switching charges. BJ FINANCES recommends evaluating all factors before proceeding.
Are there charges for a balance transfer? ▾
Most lenders charge a foreclosure fee from the existing lender and a processing fee from the new lender. These charges vary and should be factored into your calculation.
Can I get a top-up with a balance transfer? ▾
Some lenders offer a top-up loan along with a balance transfer for eligible customers. This is subject to lender policy and your credit assessment.
Does BJ FINANCES guarantee BT approval? ▾
No. BJ FINANCES assists with the balance transfer application process. Approval, rate and terms are decided solely by the new lender based on their credit assessment.
