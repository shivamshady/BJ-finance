What is a Personal Loan?

Funds for Your Personal Financial Needs

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for a variety of personal financial requirements. BJ FINANCES assists eligible customers in exploring personal loan options from banks and NBFCs.

💡 BJ FINANCES does not directly lend money. We assist customers in connecting with suitable lenders based on their profile.

Common Uses

  • Medical emergencies and healthcare expenses
  • Wedding and related family expenses
  • Education and skill development
  • Travel and holiday planning
  • Debt consolidation
  • Home renovation and improvement
  • Other personal financial requirements

General Eligibility Criteria

  • Salaried employees at private, government, MNC or PSU organisations
  • Minimum age typically 21 years (varies by lender)
  • Regular monthly salary credit via bank account
  • Minimum income as per lender requirements
  • Reasonable credit history (CIBIL score considered by lenders)
  • Employment stability as per lender policy

* Eligibility criteria, minimum income requirements and other conditions vary by lender. Final eligibility is determined by the respective bank or NBFC.

Documents Typically Required

  • KYC documents — Aadhaar, PAN Card
  • Latest 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank account statements
  • Offer letter / Employment ID
  • Photographs

* Specific documents required may vary by lender and loan amount.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BJ FINANCES guarantee loan approval? ▾
No. BJ FINANCES assists with the application process. All loan approvals, amounts, interest rates and terms are decided solely by the respective bank or NBFC based on their credit assessment and policies.
What is the minimum salary required for a personal loan? ▾
Minimum salary requirements vary by lender and city. Please share your details and our team will help identify suitable options based on your profile.
How long does the process take? ▾
Processing timelines depend on the lender, completeness of documentation and your credit profile. BJ FINANCES will keep you updated throughout the process.
What credit score is required? ▾
Credit score requirements vary by lender. A higher score generally improves eligibility prospects. Share your details and we will help assess your situation.
Does BJ FINANCES charge any fees? ▾
Please speak with our team to understand our service terms. Processing fees, if any, are charged by the respective lender and communicated transparently before proceeding.
